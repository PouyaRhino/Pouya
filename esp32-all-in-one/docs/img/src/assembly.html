Step 8 · Build it into the case

Only after every module passed its test on the bench. Stand first, then head.

STAND

  1. Glue the neck post flange under the stand top, tube leaning to the back.
  2. Radar into the front rails, antenna side to the front.
  3. AHT20 + BMP280 on the front wall (foam tape), above the vents.
  4. Battery in the bottom-lid cradle, IP5306 on foam tape on top, USB-C to the back hole.
  5. Thread the 8 neck wires up through the post, then close the bottom lid.

HEAD

  1. Display + camera into the black face plate, plate into the head, 4 dots of hot glue.
  2. Mic under the top hole, BH1750 under the window, TTP223 under the middle.
  3. Push the head onto the post, pull the neck wires up.
  4. ESP32 on its rails, USB-C to the right hole. Ribbon + display, solder the neck wires.
  5. Speaker + amp on the back lid. Run test a again, then close the lid.
Battery last. Solder the whole harness first, plug the LiPo in at the very end. Keep the iron away from it.
Lids: fix them the way the current case version says (screws or snap-fits).
